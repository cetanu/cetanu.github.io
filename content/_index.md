# Welcome to my site.

{{ image(url="/img/retro-portrait.png", alt="me", no_hover=true, end=true) }}

You might know me from a [YouTube
video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55pTFVoclvE) about being laid off after
eight years at Atlassian. In it, I talked through some of the systems I helped
build there.

I'm interested in building high-scale systems and tooling for teams, in such a
way that they are ergonomic and unlikely to be used in anger.

### What you'll find here

I occasionally publish [technical articles and short essays here](/blog), and usually
record a video to go with them. You can follow new posts through the [RSS
feed](/atom.xml).

You can see what content I plan to produce in [this kanban board](/schedule).
Half of it is random things I'm interested in, ideas, suggestions from other
people, and so on.
