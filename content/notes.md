# What kind of team I want to work on

A team where: 

* pairing/teamwork is the default
* impact is measured in nanoseconds, bytes
* volume is measured in tera, petabytes
* the challenges I work on have the opportunity to shape the industry
* there's space to innovate - make mistakes, run experiments, wade through uncertainty
* we build knowledge together and then share it with others
